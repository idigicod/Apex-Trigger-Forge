## ✨ Feature Matrix

The core strength of Apex Velocity lies in its diverse feature set, categorized into three distinct, yet interconnected, modules. Each module is engineered to solve specific tactical problems, culminating in a holistic enhancement experience.

### 📡 Module I: Perception Augmentation
- **Spatial Audio Triangulation:** Enhances the spatial resolution of audio cues, allowing for precise directional identification of footsteps, weapon fire, and healing actions from up to 300 meters away. It filters ambient noise to ensure clarity in chaotic firefights.
- **Loot Stream Prioritization:** A dynamic overlay that prioritizes nearby lootable items based on your current loadout, armor state, and inventory gaps. It visualizes the optimal looting path, reducing time spent in vulnerable inventory screens.
- **Zone Trajectory Forecast:** Utilizes historical match data and current circle movement patterns to predict the final zone location with a degree of accuracy that surpasses standard in-game predictions, enabling proactive positioning.

### ⚙️ Module II: Kinetic Response
- **Recoil Vector Stabilization:** A configurable parameter set that adjusts the recoil pattern visual feedback, allowing for more intuitive compensation, especially on high-fire-rate weapons.
- **Ballistics Luminance Tracer:** Enhances the visibility of projectile arcs and hit registration effects, making it easier to adjust for bullet drop on long-range engagements.
- **Input Latency Reduction Processor:** A suite of network and rendering tweaks that aim to reduce the perceived delay between input command and on-screen action, streamlining the control loop for peak operational responsiveness.

### 🧠 Module III: Strategic Intelligence
- **Tactical Heat Mapping:** An on-screen mini-map overlay that visualizes recent combat activity (gunfire, grenades, supply drops) to infer enemy rotations and common camping spots.
- **Engagement Timestamp Logger:** Automatically logs engagements, including time, location, and weapons used, to provide a post-match breakdown of your tactical decision-making patterns.
- **Duo/Squad Synergy Widget:** A non-intrusive sidebar that displays your team's health, ultimate cooldowns, and inventory status at a glance, fostering better coordinated pushes.

We understand that the use of enhancement frameworks raises questions about security and detection. Apex Velocity is developed with a **stealth-first philosophy**.
